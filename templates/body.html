Knowledge base (TELL)

FOL axioms (background knowledge)

∀x,y  Breeze(x,y) ⇔ ∃x′,y′ [Adjacent((x,y),(x′,y′)) ∧ Pit(x′,y′)]
∀x,y  Stench(x,y) ⇔ ∃x′,y′ [Adjacent((x,y),(x′,y′)) ∧ Wumpus(x′,y′)]
∃!x,y  Wumpus(x,y)
∀x,y  Safe(x,y) ⇔ ¬Pit(x,y) ∧ ¬Wumpus(x,y)
∀x,y  Glitter(x,y) ⇒ Gold(x,y)
For each visited cell the axioms are instantiated (universal instantiation) and added to the KB, as shown above.
Minimax: time O(bm), space O(b·m). Alpha-beta: same result, best case O(bm/2) with perfect move ordering. Wumpus World: sound and complete inference by model checking over the frontier cells.
